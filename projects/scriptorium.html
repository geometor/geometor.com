III. Historical, Epistemological & Axiomatic Scriptorium¶

Primary Philosophical, Renaissance, Axiomatic & Cosmogonic Editions

To understand what geometric objects are and how the mind knows their necessity, Pillar III publishes critical multi-language Book Codices of the fifteen thinkers and traditions that shaped the philosophy, harmonic theory, aesthetics, and axiomatics of space.

plato — Geometric & Mathematical Dialogues¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/plato

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/plato

4-Track Book Codex (Burnet Greek, Jowett English, Summary, Notes) of the Platonic mathematical canon: Meno (82b–85e, anamnesis and doubling the square via the diagonal), Republic Books VI–VIII (the Divided Line, the Quadrivium ascent from dianoia to noesis, and the Geometric Number), and Timaeus (31b–57d, the proportional World-Soul scale and elemental right triangles composing the five cosmic solids), paired with exact geometor.model constructions.

aristotle — Loci Geometrici et Noetici¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/aristotle

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/aristotle

Curated multi-track edition (Bekker Greek, Oxford English, Summary, Notes) of the foundational Aristotelian passages on demonstrative science (Posterior Analytics), the continuum and infinity (Physics), geometric actualization by construction (Metaphysics IX.9), mathematical abstraction and phantasia (De Anima III), and celestial spheres (De Caelo).

nicomachus — Introduction to Arithmetic (Ἀριθμητικὴ Εἰσαγωγή)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/nicomachus

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/nicomachus

4-Track Book Codex (Hoche 1866 Greek, D’Ooge 1926 English, Summary, Notes) of Nicomachus of Gerasa’s Neo-Pythagorean treatise (Books I–II): the canonical division of Multitude and Magnitude into the Four *Mathemata* (*Quadrivium*), Nicomachus’s Rule of Three Terms unfolding every species of inequality deductively from pure Equality \((1, 1, 1)\), polygonal gnomon lattices, the sum-of-cubes theorem, the ten classical means, and the “Most Perfect” Cube Proportion \(6 : 8 :: 9 : 12\).

proclus — Commentary on Book I of Euclid’s Elements¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/proclus

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/proclus

4-Track Book Codex (English, Friedlein Greek 1873, Summary, Notes) of Proclus Lycius’s fifth-century philosophical and mathematical commentary on Euclid Book I, paired with a 164-locus concordance. Preserves the Eudemian history of Greek geometry, the six classical parts of a Euclidean proposition (protasis, ekthesis, diorismos, kataskeue, apodeixis, symperasma), and the doctrine of imaginative projection (probole) onto the screen of phantasia.

fibonacci — Liber Abaci (1202) & Practica Geometriae (1220)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/fibonacci

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/fibonacci

4-Track Book Codex (Boncompagni 1857–1862 Latin, Original English, Summary, Notes) of Leonardo Pisano’s dual masterworks transmitting Hindu–Arabic numerals, continued-fraction unit-fraction decompositions, the rabbit recurrence \(F_{n} = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}\), algebraic Regula Rectae equations (Ch. 15), and exact Euclidean/Ptolemaic measurement of the regular pentagon, decagon, and icosahedron over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})\).

aquinas — Opera de Scientia Mathematica et Materia Intelligibili¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/aquinas

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/aquinas

4-Track Book Codex (Latin, English, Summary, Notes) across 5 Parts and 37 sections collecting St. Thomas Aquinas’s decisive texts on the epistemology of mathematics—centered on Super Boetium De Trinitate (qq. 5–6) on intelligible matter (materia intelligibilis) and terminatio ad imaginationem, alongside selections from In Posteriora Analytica, In De Anima, In Physicorum, and In Metaphysicorum, cross-linked via a 78-locus Hoenen concordance.

cusa — Nicholas of Cusa’s De Docta Ignorantia (1440)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/cusa

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/cusa

4-Track Book Codex (1514 Paris / Heidelberg Latin, Original English, Summary, Notes) of Book I of Cardinal Nicholas of Cusa’s On Learned Ignorance, modeling the Coincidence of Opposites (coincidentia oppositorum) through exact limiting transformations in geometor.model—where an infinite circle coincides with its straight tangent, an infinite triangle collapses into a line, and center and circumference become one.

pacioli — Fra Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509) & Summa (1494)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/pacioli · Summa de Arithmetica (1494)

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/pacioli

Multi-track scholarly codices of Fra Luca Pacioli’s two Venetian monuments:

  • *De Divina Proportione* (1509 / Winterberg 1889): 5-Track Codex (English, Italiano 1509, Deutsch 1889, Latin/Greek, Notes) spanning 318 archival folios and 482 recalibrated woodcuts, including the thirteen effects of the Divine Proportion, Roman architectural letterforms, Piero della Francesca’s Libellus, and all 60 solid and skeletal polyhedra plates drawn by Leonardo da Vinci.

  • *Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita* (1494): 4-Track Codex (English, Italiano 1494, Summary, Notes) covering speculative arithmetic, Cossic surds, Boethian/Euclidean proportions, Venetian double-entry Quaderno, and the Tractatus Geometriae.

durer — Albrecht Dürer’s Underweysung der Messung (1525)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/durer

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/durer

4-Track Book Codex (1525 Nuremberg Early New High German, Original English, Summary, Notes) of Dürer’s four-book manual of constructive geometry for artists and architects: Archimedean and logarithmic shell spirals, conic sections by parallel descriptive projection, Dürer’s celebrated rusty-compass pentagon (fünfeck mit unverrücktem Zirkel), unfolded polyhedral nets (Netze), modular Roman & Fraktur typography, and linear perspective machines.

kepler — Kepler’s Mysterium (1596), Strena (1611) & Harmonices Mundi (1619)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/kepler

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/kepler

4-Track Bilingual Book Codex (Latin, Original English, Summary, Notes) + exact SymPy / geometor.model engine uniting Johannes Kepler’s geometric trilogy: the five nested Platonic solids separating the six Copernican planetary spheres (Mysterium Cosmographicum), the six-cornered snowflake, rhombic dodecahedron, sphere-packing conjecture \(\pi/(3\sqrt{2})\), and Fibonacci phyllotaxis (Strena), and constructive polygon epistemology (Scibilia vs. Inscibilia), the 11 Archimedean tilings, the Kepler Right Triangle \((1, \sqrt{\phi}, \phi)\), and the Harmonic Third Law \(T^2 \propto a^3\) (Harmonices Mundi).

hilbert — David Hilbert’s Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899/1902)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/hilbert

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/hilbert

Multi-track edition (German original, E. J. Townsend English translation, and metamathematical commentary) of David Hilbert’s Foundations of Geometry—reorganizing Euclidean geometry into five rigorous axiom groups (Incidence, Order, Congruence, Parallels, Continuity), building the algebra of segments from Desargues’s and Pascal’s theorems, and proving axiom consistency and independence through explicit algebraic coordinate fields.

poincare — Henri Poincaré’s Science and Hypothesis (1902/1905)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/poincare

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/poincare

4-Track Bilingual Book Codex (1902 Flammarion French, 1905 Halsted/Greenstreet English, Summary, Notes) of Part II (Space) of La Science et l’Hypothèse, paired with exact symbolic Möbius/conformal disk models proving the relative consistency of Lobachevskian hyperbolic geometry via Poincaré’s famous Euclidean–hyperbolic dictionary.

bertrand-russell — Foundations of Geometry & Principles of Mathematics¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/bertrand-russell

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/bertrand-russell

Scholarly edition of Bertrand Russell’s two foundational works on geometric space—An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry (1897) and Part VI (Space) of The Principles of Mathematics (1903)—examining the philosophical transition from Kantian intuition through projective and Cayley–Klein non-Euclidean geometries to pure relational logic. (Distinct from Walter & Lao Russell’s cosmogonic edition below.)

hoenen — Petrus Hoenen, S.J.’s De Noetica Geometriae (1954)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/hoenen

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/hoenen

First complete digital edition and facing Latin–English translation of Fr. Petrus Hoenen, S.J.’s De Noetica Geometriae Origine Theoriae Cognitionis (Analecta Gregoriana, Rome, 1954). Answering modern axiomatic formalism, Hoenen demonstrates through Aristotle, Euclid, Proclus, Aquinas, Hilbert, and Russell that universal geometric nexus is first grasped through intuitive formal abstraction directly within constructed spatial continua.

russell — Walter & Lao Russell’s Home Study Course (1950–1952)¶

  • Live Book Codex: geometor.github.io/russell

  • Repository: github.com/geometor/russell

Complete 12-Unit, 48-Lesson digital codex of Walter and Lao Russell’s Home Study Course in Universal Law, Natural Science and Living Philosophy, featuring 180 vector-reconstructed scientific diagrams and interactive 3D visualizations of the Russells’ cube-sphere optical wave-field cosmogony.

Projects¶

The Five Pillars & Complete 46-Repository Atlas

The GEOMETOR ecosystem spans forty-six public research repositories—uniting Scholarly Editions (*Book Codices*) of foundational public-domain treatises with Mathematical Catalogs of modern constructive research (etc, wildberger, divine, knott, codes)—organized into five interconnected pillars and grounded in exact symbolic algebra (SymPy) and photon-platform/publish.

Ecosystem Overview¶

  • I. The Symbolic Core, Discovery & Software Catalog — model, explorer, divine, codes, trees

  • II. The Unfolding Canon (The Elementrium & Constructive Geometry) — euclid, elements, byrne, archimedes, apollonius, heron, menelaus, ptolemy, pappus, ceva, feuerbach, staudt, petersen, casey, lemoine, klein, altshiller-court, fundamentals, etc, wildberger

  • III. Historical, Epistemological & Axiomatic Scriptorium — plato, aristotle, nicomachus, proclus, fibonacci, aquinas, cusa, pacioli, durer, kepler, hilbert, poincare, bertrand-russell, hoenen, russell

  • IV. Living Geometry & Morphogenesis — phyllotaxis, thompson, knott

  • V. Machine Reasoning & Program Synthesis — seer, seer_navigator, seer_sessions

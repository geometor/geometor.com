Projects¶

The Five Pillars & Complete Repository Atlas

The GEOMETOR ecosystem spans twenty-three public research repositories and scholarly Book Codices organized into five interconnected pillars—all grounded in exact symbolic algebra (SymPy) and published through photon-platform/publish.

Ecosystem Overview¶

  • I. The Symbolic Core & Discovery Engine — model, explorer, divine, trees

  • II. The Unfolding Canon (The Elementrium) — euclid, elements, fundamentals, altshiller-court, etc, pappus, wildberger

  • III. Historical, Epistemological & Axiomatic Scriptorium — aristotle, proclus, aquinas, pacioli, hilbert, bertrand-russell, hoenen, russell

  • IV. Living Geometry & Morphogenesis — phyllotaxis

  • V. Machine Reasoning & Program Synthesis — seer, seer_navigator, seer_sessions

Projects¶

The Five Pillars & Complete 61-Repository Atlas

The GEOMETOR ecosystem spans sixty-one public research repositories—uniting 74 multi-track Book Codices across 39 historical authors with 11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs, interactive WebGL studios, and neuro-symbolic reasoning tools—grounded in exact symbolic algebra (SymPy) and photon-platform/publish.

Tip

Looking directly for a specific treatise or catalog? Visit the Books & Catalogs Directory for direct links to all 74 Book Codices and 11 Mathematical Catalogs.

Ecosystem Overview¶

  • I. The Symbolic Core, Discovery & Software Catalog — model, explorer, divine, polyhedra, codes, elementarium, press, animations, trees (plus geometor.com)

  • II. The Unfolding Canon (The Elementrium & Constructive Geometry) — euclid, elements, byrne, archimedes, apollonius, heron, menelaus, ptolemy, pappus, pascal, ceva, euler, mascheroni, feuerbach, steiner, staudt, petersen, casey, klein, lemoine, altshiller-court, fundamentals, etc, wildberger

  • III. Historical, Epistemological & Axiomatic Scriptorium — plato, aristotle, nicomachus, proclus, fibonacci, aquinas, cusa, piero, pacioli, durer, kepler, pasch, hilbert, poincare, bertrand-russell, hoenen, russell

  • IV. Living Geometry & Morphogenesis — phyllotaxis, thompson, hambidge, knott, huntley, livio, posamentier

  • V. Machine Reasoning & Program Synthesis — seer, seer_navigator, seer_sessions

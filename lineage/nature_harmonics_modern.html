Living Form, Harmonics & Symbolic Catalogs Today¶

Haeckel · D’Arcy Thompson · Kayser · Walter & Lao Russell · Odom · Jean · Bogomolny · Knott · Kimberling · Wildberger

Ernst Haeckel (1834–1919)¶

Naturalist, marine biologist, and scientific illustrator whose Kunstformen der Natur (Art Forms in Nature, 1899–1904) revealed icosahedral, dodecahedral, hexagonal, and logarithmic spiral geometries in radiolarians, diatoms, siphonophores, and calcareous sponges—demonstrating that polyhedral symmetry is an active morphogenetic principle of living organisms.

Sir D’Arcy Wentworth Thompson (1860–1948)¶

  • GEOMETOR Edition: thompson — On Growth and Form (1917) (Repo)

Scottish biologist, mathematician, and classical scholar whose On Growth and Form (1917) revealed physical forces and geometric invariance across living structures: Plateau’s \(120^\circ\) and \(\arccos(-1/3)\) minimal-surface junctions, Maraldi’s rhombic honeycomb apex, gnomonic logarithmic shells (Nautilus, Ammonites, Foraminifera), and Cartesian coordinate transformations relating homologous biological species.

Hans Kayser (1891–1964)¶

Founder of modern Pythagorean Harmonics (Akróasis: The Theory of World Harmonics, Lehrbuch der Harmonik). Kayser investigated integer and superparticular string ratios, the lambdoma matrix, crystallographic proportions, and botanical forms—inspiring GEOMETOR’s union of visual construction with pure-interval acoustic cues in @geometor studio broadcasts.

Walter Russell (1871–1963) & Lao Russell (1904–1988)¶

  • GEOMETOR Edition: russell — Home Study Course (Repo)

Artist, sculptor, and natural philosopher Walter Russell and co-author Lao Russell articulated a geometric wave-field cosmogony built on the interpenetrating cube and sphere—modeling compression and expansion as rhythmic optical projections between cubic zero-curvature boundaries and spherical wave-centers across 180 reconstructed vector figures.

George Odom (1941–2010)¶

  • Featured on: GEOMETOR (Interactive Hero Construction) · divine — Golden Ratio Catalog

Artist and geometer who discovered one of the simplest golden section constructions in history (published as Problem E3007 in the American Mathematical Monthly, 1983, proved by H. S. M. Coxeter): joining the midpoints \(A\) and \(B\) of two sides of an equilateral triangle and extending \([A B]\) to its circumcircle cuts the chord in Extreme and Mean Ratio \(\phi\)—the exact climax of the Vesica-to-\(\phi\) figure on the threshold of geometor.com.

Roger V. Jean¶

  • Featured in: phyllotaxis

Mathematician whose treatise Phyllotaxis: A Systemic Study in Plant Morphogenesis (Cambridge University Press, 1994) unified continued-fraction divergence theory, visible opposed parastichy pairs, allometric growth matrices, and entropy-minimization models—directly informing the symbolic solvers and 2D/3D WebGL meristem studios in geometor/phyllotaxis.

Alexander Bogomolny (1948–2018) — Cut-the-Knot¶

  • GEOMETOR Catalog: divine — Golden Ratio Constructions Catalog (Repo)

Mathematician and master expositor whose encyclopedic portal Interactive Mathematics Miscellany and Puzzles (Cut-the-Knot) curated dozens of elegant golden section constructions discovered by Odom, Hofstetter, Tran Quang Hung, Bataille, and Lemoine—systematically documented and proved over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) in geometor/divine.

Ron Knott — Fibonacci Numbers & the Golden Section¶

  • GEOMETOR Catalog: knott — Fibonacci, Golden Section & Wythoff Catalog (Repo)

Mathematician at the University of Surrey whose acclaimed educational corpus connected Fibonacci recurrences, Lucas identities, Pisano modular periods, Pythagorean triple generators, Zeckendorf representations, golden gnomons, and Wythoff’s game—documented one fact per page with exact SymPy and geometor.model constructions in geometor/knott.

Clark Kimberling & Forum Geometricorum¶

  • GEOMETOR Catalog: etc — Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (Repo · YouTube Playlist)

Through the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC, initiated 1994/1998 and now surpassing 53,000 centers) and the open-access journal Forum Geometricorum (founded by Paul Yiu), Clark Kimberling and collaborators transformed classical triangle geometry into a systematic algebraic discipline—cataloged across 4,000+ compiled centers and 16,000+ interactive SVGs in geometor/etc.

Norman J. Wildberger¶

  • GEOMETOR Catalog: wildberger — Divine Proportions (Repo) · geometor.model.invariants

Author of Divine Proportions: Rational Trigonometry to Universal Geometry (2005). By replacing distance with quadrance \(Q\) and transcendental angle with spread \(s\), Wildberger stripped trigonometry of infinite power series and floating-point approximations, allowing Euclidean, relativistic, and projective geometries—as well as ADE graph mutation dynamics—to be computed purely algebraically over arbitrary fields.

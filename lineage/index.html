Lineage¶

Masters, Epistemologists, Geometers & Naturalists Behind GEOMETOR

GEOMETOR does not invent a private geometry; it stands within a 2,400-year lineage of geometers, philosophers, artists, astronomers, botanists, and algebraists who recognized form and proportion as an intelligible bridge between nature and mind.

What began in 2020–2021 as a reading list of study notes has grown into a living library of 74 multi-track Book Codices and 11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs (browse all Books & Catalogs), where nearly every thinker below is represented by a dedicated scholarly edition or executable geometor.model laboratory across our 61 repositories.

Chronological & Thematic Gallery¶

  • Classical Antiquity: Demonstration, Elements, Conics & Projection — Plato, Aristotle, Euclid, Archimedes, Apollonius of Perga, Heron of Alexandria, Menelaus of Alexandria, Nicomachus of Gerasa, Claudius Ptolemy, Pappus of Alexandria, Proclus Lycius

  • Medieval Transmission, Renaissance Proportion & Classical Triangle Statics — Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci), Thomas Aquinas, Nicholas of Cusa, Piero della Francesca, Fra Luca Pacioli & Leonardo da Vinci, Albrecht Dürer, Johannes Kepler, Blaise Pascal, Giovanni Ceva, Leonhard Euler, Lorenzo Mascheroni, Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach

  • Modern Axiomatics, Synthetic Mastery & Geometric Noetics — Jakob Steiner, Oliver Byrne, Karl Georg Christian von Staudt, Julius Petersen, Moritz Pasch, John Casey, Émile Lemoine, Felix Klein, Henri Poincaré, David Hilbert, Bertrand Russell, Nathan Altshiller-Court, Petrus Hoenen, S.J., Fred W. Seelig

  • Living Form, Harmonics & Symbolic Catalogs Today — Ernst Haeckel, D’Arcy Wentworth Thompson, Jay Hambidge, Hans Kayser, Walter & Lao Russell, H. E. Huntley, George Odom, Roger V.

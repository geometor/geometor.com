Lineage¶

Masters, Epistemologists, Geometers & Naturalists Behind GEOMETOR

GEOMETOR does not invent a private geometry; it stands within a 2,300-year lineage of geometers, philosophers, artists, botanists, and algebraists who recognized form and proportion as an intelligible bridge between nature and mind.

Remarkably, what began in 2020–2021 as a reading list of study notes has grown into a living library of multi-track Book Codices and symbolic Python engines, where nearly every thinker below is represented by a dedicated scholarly edition or constructive laboratory.

Chronological & Thematic Gallery¶

  • Classical Antiquity: Demonstration, Elements & Projection — Aristotle, Euclid, Pappus of Alexandria, Proclus Lycius

  • Scholastic Noetics, Renaissance Proportion & Celestial Harmony — Thomas Aquinas, Fra Luca Pacioli & Leonardo da Vinci, Johannes Kepler, Blaise Pascal

  • Modern Axiomatics, Synthetic Mastery & Geometric Noetics — David Hilbert, Bertrand Russell, Nathan Altshiller-Court, Petrus Hoenen, S.J., Fred W. Seelig

  • Living Form, Harmonics & Algebraic Geometry Today — Ernst Haeckel, Hans Kayser, Walter & Lao Russell, Roger V. Jean, George Odom, Clark Kimberling (Forum Geometricorum), Norman J. Wildberger

The 61-Repository Library: 74 Working Book Codices & 11 Mathematical Catalogs¶

November 16, 2026 — With release v0.4.0, GEOMETOR launches the unified Books & Catalogs Directory on geometor.com/books/, indexing 74 multi-track historical Book Codices across 39 standalone author scriptoria alongside 11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs across 61 public repositories.

1. Standalone Author Scriptoria & Unbundled Treatises¶

Every historical master in the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium now inhabits a dedicated author repository where each treatise is a first-class :type: book edition with canonical chapter/proposition structure and synchronized tracks:

  • Newly Established Standalone Editions: piero (Libellus de Quinque Corporibus Regularibus), pascal (Essay pour les Coniques, 1640), euler (Solutio facilis, E325, 1765), mascheroni (La Geometria del Compasso, 1797), steiner (Die geometrischen Constructionen, 1833), pasch (Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie, 1882), and hambidge (The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry, 1920).

  • Multi-Book Author Scriptoria: Direct treatise-level navigation across plato (5 dialogues), aristotle (5 treatises), euclid (Elements & Data), archimedes (7 treatises), apollonius (2 books), heron (4 treatises), ptolemy (2 books), fibonacci (2 books), aquinas (6 works), cusa (2 books), pacioli (2 books), kepler (3 books), staudt (2 books), klein (2 books), bertrand-russell (2 books), and russell (2 books), backed by 8,430+ tracked 300-DPI primary-source folio scans.

2. Eleven Symbolic Mathematical Catalogs & Platform Archives¶

  • Expanded Golden Ratio & Polyhedral Catalogs: Added polyhedra (Catalog of Polyhedra & Leonardo’s 1509 Plates), huntley (The Divine Proportion), livio (The Golden Ratio & Myth Triage), and posamentier (The Glorious Golden Ratio), alongside elements, etc, wildberger, fundamentals, divine, knott, and codes.

  • Optical Preservation & Publishing: Added elementarium (100,000-year passive optical bootstrap archive) and press (Digital Scriptorium master shelf & design system).

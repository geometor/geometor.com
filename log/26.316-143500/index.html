Scholarly Editions & Mathematical Catalogs: The 46-Repository Canon¶

November 12, 2026 — With release v0.3.0, GEOMETOR completes its Wave 2 expansion to 46 interconnected repositories, establishing a clear architectural complementarity between Scholarly Editions (*Book Codices*) for classical treatises and Mathematical Catalogs for contemporary mathematical corpora.

1. Twenty-Two New & Expanded Classical Book Codices¶

  • Alexandrian & Classical Canon: plato (Meno, Republic, Timaeus), archimedes (Opera Geometrica), apollonius (Conics & On Tangencies), heron (Metrica & Catoptrica), menelaus (Sphaerica), nicomachus (Introduction to Arithmetic), ptolemy (Almagest Book I & Planisphaerium), expanded euclid (Data & Optics), and expanded pappus (Synagoge Books III–VIII).

  • Medieval, Renaissance & Celestial Geometry: fibonacci (Liber Abaci & Practica Geometriae), cusa (De Docta Ignorantia), expanded pacioli (Summa 1494), durer (Underweysung der Messung 1525), kepler (Mysterium, Strena, & Harmonices Mundi), and ceva (Statica Constructio 1678).

  • Modern Triangle, Projective, Visual & Morphological Classics: feuerbach (Ceva–Euler–Feuerbach trilogy), byrne (1847 polychrome visual pseudocode), staudt (Geometrie der Lage 1847), petersen (Methods and Theories 1879), casey (Sequel to Euclid 1888), lemoine (Géométrographie 1902), klein (Erlangen Program & Famous Problems), poincare (Science and Hypothesis), thompson (On Growth and Form 1917), and reparsed one-item-per-page altshiller-court (College Geometry 1925).

2. Five One-Item-Per-Page Symbolic Mathematical Catalogs¶

For living encyclopedias and copyrighted modern works, GEOMETOR documents the underlying mathematical facts one item per page using 100% original prose, SymPy proofs, and geometor.model vector diagrams:

  • codes — Unified Cross-Corpus Notation & Symbolic Code Registry

  • divine — Catalog of Golden Ratio Constructions (Euclid, Ptolemy, Odom, Hofstetter, Hung, Bataille, Cut-the-Knot)

  • knott — Catalog of Ron Knott’s Fibonacci Numbers, the Golden Section & Wythoff Geometry

  • etc — Catalog of Clark Kimberling’s Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (expanded with 9 classical structural theorems & Euler/Nagel/Brocard/Gergonne lines)

  • wildberger — Catalog of Norman J. Wildberger’s Rational Trigonometry

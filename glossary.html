Glossary¶

Core Vocabulary of the GEOMETOR Architecture

Book Codex¶

GEOMETOR’s multi-track digital scholarly format powered by photon-platform/publish, pairing synchronized source languages, formal pseudocode, executable Python, and interactive vector figures on every folio.

Circle¶

A closed planar projection ( A B ) swept by rotating radius point B perpendicularly around stationary center A (G.10, G.15). Unlike [ A B ], ( A B ) and ( B A ) are distinct loci.

Cross-Ratio¶

The fundamental four-point projective invariant \((A, B; C, D) = \frac{(A - C)(B - D)}{(A - D)(B - C)}\), preserved under central projection (geometor/pappus).

Equidistant Poles¶

The conjugate intersection points \(C\) and \(C'\) of two symmetric circles ( A B ) and ( B A ), each equidistant from A and B and defining the orthogonal axis [ C C′ ] perpendicular to [ A B ].

Extreme and Mean Ratio¶

Euclid’s classical name (Book VI, Def. 3) for the Golden Ratio or Divine Proportion \(\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\), satisfying \(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{b}{a + b}\) and \(\phi^2 - \phi - 1 = 0\).

G-Index¶

The 601-node topological dependency sequence (G.1–G.601) in geometor/elements ordering Euclid’s definitions, postulates, common notions, and propositions into a strict directed acyclic graph.

Golden Chain¶

A sequence of collinear segments on a constructed line whose successive lengths form a geometric progression in powers of \(\phi\) (\(\dots, \phi^{-1}, 1, \phi, \phi^2, \dots\)) while simultaneously obeying the additive Fibonacci recurrence \(\phi^{n+1} = \phi^n + \phi^{n-1}\).

Harmonic Range¶

Four collinear points \(A, B, C, D\) whose cross-ratio equals \((A, B; C, D) = -1\), meaning \(C\) and \(D\) divide segment / A B / internally and externally in the same ratio. Constructible by unmarked straightedge alone from a complete quadrangle.

Intelligible Matter¶

Materia intelligibilis in Aristotle and Thomas Aquinas (geometor/aquinas): the pure continuum of spatial extension abstracted from sensible qualities, in which geometric figures are constructed and judged.

Intuitive Formal Abstraction¶

Fr. Petrus Hoenen’s epistemological principle (geometor/hoenen) that universal geometric necessity is apprehended directly within concrete spatial construction prior to formal axiomatic codification.

Line¶

An infinite straight projection [ A B ] determined symmetrically by two distinct points (G.2). Expressed algebraically as \(ax + by + c = 0\) with exact symbolic coefficients.

Point¶

An indivisible location A on the planar field (Euclid I, Def. 1 / G.1). In geometor.model, a point represents exact algebraic information \((x, y)\)—either placed as one of the two initial givens or selected by the intersection of two constructed loci.

Quadrance¶

In Rational Trigonometry (geometor/wildberger), the exact algebraic square of separation between two points, \(Q(A_1, A_2) = (x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2\), avoiding unnecessary square roots.

Segment¶

The bounded portion / A B / of a line [ A B ] lying between extremities A and B (G.4).

Spread¶

In Rational Trigonometry, the exact rational measure of inclination between two lines, \(s(\ell_1, \ell_2) \in [0, 1]\) (equivalent to \(\sin^2\theta\) in Euclidean space), where \(s = 0\) is parallel, \(s = \tfrac{1}{2}\) is \(45^\circ\), \(s = \tfrac{3}{4}\) is \(60^\circ\), and \(s = 1\) is perpendicular.

Vesica Piscis¶

The symmetric lens formed by two congruent circles ( A B ) and ( B A ) each passing through the other’s center. Their intersections select the Equidistant Poles, generating the equilateral triangle, perpendicular bisection, and \(\sqrt{3}\).

Projects
========

.. rubric:: The Five Pillars & Complete 46-Repository Atlas

The **GEOMETOR** ecosystem spans **forty-six** public research repositories—uniting **Scholarly Editions (*Book Codices*)** of foundational public-domain treatises with **Mathematical Catalogs** of modern constructive research (``etc``, ``wildberger``, ``divine``, ``knott``, ``codes``)—organized into five interconnected pillars and grounded in exact symbolic algebra (`SymPy <https://www.sympy.org/>`_) and `photon-platform/publish <https://github.com/photon-platform/publish>`_.

Ecosystem Overview
------------------

* :doc:`core` — ``model``, ``explorer``, ``divine``, ``codes``, ``trees``
* :doc:`elementrium` — ``euclid``, ``elements``, ``byrne``, ``archimedes``, ``apollonius``, ``heron``, ``menelaus``, ``ptolemy``, ``pappus``, ``ceva``, ``feuerbach``, ``staudt``, ``petersen``, ``casey``, ``lemoine``, ``klein``, ``altshiller-court``, ``fundamentals``, ``etc``, ``wildberger``
* :doc:`scriptorium` — ``plato``, ``aristotle``, ``nicomachus``, ``proclus``, ``fibonacci``, ``aquinas``, ``cusa``, ``pacioli``, ``durer``, ``kepler``, ``hilbert``, ``poincare``, ``bertrand-russell``, ``hoenen``, ``russell``
* :doc:`living_geometry` — ``phyllotaxis``, ``thompson``, ``knott``
* :doc:`reasoning` — ``seer``, ``seer_navigator``, ``seer_sessions``

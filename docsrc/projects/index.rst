Projects
========

.. rubric:: The Five Pillars & Complete Repository Atlas

The **GEOMETOR** ecosystem spans twenty-three public research repositories and scholarly Book Codices organized into five interconnected pillars—all grounded in exact symbolic algebra (`SymPy <https://www.sympy.org/>`_) and published through `photon-platform/publish <https://github.com/photon-platform/publish>`_.

Ecosystem Overview
------------------

* :doc:`core` — ``model``, ``explorer``, ``divine``, ``trees``
* :doc:`elementrium` — ``euclid``, ``elements``, ``fundamentals``, ``altshiller-court``, ``etc``, ``pappus``, ``wildberger``
* :doc:`scriptorium` — ``aristotle``, ``proclus``, ``aquinas``, ``pacioli``, ``hilbert``, ``bertrand-russell``, ``hoenen``, ``russell``
* :doc:`living_geometry` — ``phyllotaxis``
* :doc:`reasoning` — ``seer``, ``seer_navigator``, ``seer_sessions``

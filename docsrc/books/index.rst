Books & Catalogs
================

.. rubric:: Complete Index of GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium Editions & Mathematical Catalogs

Across **39 author scriptoria** and **11 constructive catalog archives**, GEOMETOR publishes **74 multi-track historical Book Codices** and **11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs**—each grounded in exact `SymPy <https://www.sympy.org/>`_ algebra, archival folio provenance, and calibrated theme-adaptive ``.euclid-graphic`` vector diagrams.

* **Mathematical Catalogs (11)**: Structured one-item-per-page symbolic compendia documenting classical dependency graphs, triangle centers, rational trigonometry laws, polyhedra, golden ratio constructions, Fibonacci identities, and geometric theorem-proving engines.
* **Historical & Philosophical Book Codices (74 Books · 39 Authors)**: Facing-language critical editions (*Greek, Latin, Italian, Early New High German, German, French, Danish/English*) preserving verbatim historical primary texts alongside scholarly summaries, notes, concordances, and over **8,400 tracked 300-DPI primary-source folio scans**.

III. Symbolic Engines, Studios, Archive Architecture & Reasoning
----------------------------------------------------------------

Alongside our Book Codices and Mathematical Catalogs, the remaining **10 repositories** (plus ``geometor.com``) provide the symbolic engine, interactive WebGL laboratories, archival preservation, and program-synthesis tools powering the ecosystem:

* **Symbolic Core & Visualization**: `model <https://geometor.github.io/model/>`_ (exact ``SymPy`` kernel) · `explorer <https://geometor.github.io/explorer/>`_ (interactive SVG workbench) · `animations <https://geometor.github.io/animations/>`_ (golden-section proportional studies) · `trees <https://geometor.github.io/trees/>`_ (Stern–Brocot & monotone planar tree drawings)
* **Living Geometry, Preservation & Publishing**: `phyllotaxis <https://geometor.github.io/phyllotaxis/>`_ (`2D Meristem <https://geometor.github.io/phyllotaxis/demos/meristem_2d.html>`_ · `3D Propagation <https://geometor.github.io/phyllotaxis/demos/propagation_3d.html>`_) · `elementarium <https://geometor.github.io/elementarium/>`_ (100,000-year passive optical bootstrap archive) · `press <https://geometor.github.io/press/>`_ (Digital Scriptorium master shelf & print design system)
* **Machine Reasoning & Program Synthesis**: `seer <https://geometor.github.io/seer/>`_ (neuro-symbolic ARC-AGI Python synthesis) · `seer_navigator <https://geometor.github.io/seer_navigator/>`_ (``Textual`` TUI trajectory browser) · `seer_sessions <https://geometor.github.io/seer_sessions/>`_ (longitudinal reasoning observatory across 8,514 runs)

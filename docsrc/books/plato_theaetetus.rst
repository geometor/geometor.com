Theaetetus
==========

.. image:: /_static/heroes/plato.svg
   :alt: Theaetetus

Theodorus of Cyrene constructs the spiral of square roots from 3 to 17 square feet, leading Theaetetus and the younger Socrates to generalize the classification of lengths (mēkē) vs. powers (dynameis) (147d–148b).

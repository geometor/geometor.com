Lineage
=======

.. rubric:: Masters, Epistemologists, Geometers & Naturalists Behind GEOMETOR

GEOMETOR does not invent a private geometry; it stands within a 2,400-year lineage of geometers, philosophers, artists, astronomers, botanists, and algebraists who recognized **form and proportion** as an intelligible bridge between nature and mind.

What began in 2020–2021 as a reading list of study notes has grown into a living library of **74 multi-track Book Codices** and **11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs** (:doc:`browse all Books & Catalogs <../books/index>`), where nearly every thinker below is represented by a dedicated scholarly edition or executable ``geometor.model`` laboratory across our 61 repositories.

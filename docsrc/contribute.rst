Contribute
==========

.. rubric:: Collaborating on Symbolic Constructions, Codices & Proofs

GEOMETOR welcomes thoughtful contributions from geometers, historians of mathematics and philosophy, classicists, botanists, and Python developers.

Ways to Contribute
------------------

1. **Symbolic Constructions & Invariant Proofs**
   Contribute new compass-and-straightedge constructions, golden section discoveries, projective configurations, or triangle center scripts in `model <https://github.com/geometor/model>`_, `divine <https://github.com/geometor/divine>`_, `elements <https://github.com/geometor/elements>`_, `etc <https://github.com/geometor/etc>`_, `pappus <https://github.com/geometor/pappus>`_, or `wildberger <https://github.com/geometor/wildberger>`_.

2. **Scholarly Text & Translation Refinement**
   Report diplomatic transcription errata, folio alignment notes, `:g:` diagram bindings, or commentary improvements across our primary Book Codices (`euclid <https://github.com/geometor/euclid>`_, `proclus <https://github.com/geometor/proclus>`_, `aristotle <https://github.com/geometor/aristotle>`_, `aquinas <https://github.com/geometor/aquinas>`_, `pacioli <https://github.com/geometor/pacioli>`_, `hilbert <https://github.com/geometor/hilbert>`_, `bertrand-russell <https://github.com/geometor/bertrand-russell>`_, `hoenen <https://github.com/geometor/hoenen>`_, `altshiller-court <https://github.com/geometor/altshiller-court>`_).

3. **Core Engineering Principles**
   - **Exact Symbolic Algebra**: Use exact ``SymPy`` expressions (`sp.Rational`, `sp.sqrt`, algebraic roots) rather than floating-point approximations for geometric truth tests.
   - **Clean Python 3.13 & ``uv``**: Format and lint with ``ruff`` and include ``pytest`` verification for algebraic assertions.

To start a conversation or propose an idea before opening a pull request, visit `GEOMETOR GitHub Discussions <https://github.com/orgs/geometor/discussions>`_.

The 61-Repository Library: 74 Working Book Codices & 11 Mathematical Catalogs
=============================================================================

*November 16, 2026* — With release ``v0.4.0``, **GEOMETOR** launches the unified :doc:`Books & Catalogs Directory <../../books/index>` on `geometor.com/books/ <https://geometor.com/books/>`_, indexing **74 multi-track historical Book Codices** across **39 standalone author scriptoria** alongside **11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs** across **61 public repositories**.

1. Standalone Author Scriptoria & Unbundled Treatises
-----------------------------------------------------

Every historical master in the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium now inhabits a dedicated author repository where each treatise is a first-class ``:type: book`` edition with canonical chapter/proposition structure and synchronized tracks:

* **Newly Established Standalone Editions**: `piero <https://geometor.github.io/piero/quinque_corporibus/>`_ (*Libellus de Quinque Corporibus Regularibus*), `pascal <https://geometor.github.io/pascal/essay_coniques_1640/>`_ (*Essay pour les Coniques*, 1640), `euler <https://geometor.github.io/euler/solutio_facilis_1765/>`_ (*Solutio facilis*, *E325*, 1765), `mascheroni <https://geometor.github.io/mascheroni/geometria_del_compasso/>`_ (*La Geometria del Compasso*, 1797), `steiner <https://geometor.github.io/steiner/constructionen_1833/>`_ (*Die geometrischen Constructionen*, 1833), `pasch <https://geometor.github.io/pasch/vorlesungen_1882/>`_ (*Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie*, 1882), and `hambidge <https://geometor.github.io/hambidge/dynamic_symmetry/>`_ (*The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry*, 1920).
* **Multi-Book Author Scriptoria**: Direct treatise-level navigation across `plato <https://geometor.github.io/plato/>`_ (5 dialogues), `aristotle <https://geometor.github.io/aristotle/>`_ (5 treatises), `euclid <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/>`_ (*Elements* & *Data*), `archimedes <https://geometor.github.io/archimedes/>`_ (7 treatises), `apollonius <https://geometor.github.io/apollonius/>`_ (2 books), `heron <https://geometor.github.io/heron/>`_ (4 treatises), `ptolemy <https://geometor.github.io/ptolemy/>`_ (2 books), `fibonacci <https://geometor.github.io/fibonacci/>`_ (2 books), `aquinas <https://geometor.github.io/aquinas/>`_ (6 works), `cusa <https://geometor.github.io/cusa/>`_ (2 books), `pacioli <https://geometor.github.io/pacioli/>`_ (2 books), `kepler <https://geometor.github.io/kepler/>`_ (3 books), `staudt <https://geometor.github.io/staudt/>`_ (2 books), `klein <https://geometor.github.io/klein/>`_ (2 books), `bertrand-russell <https://geometor.github.io/bertrand-russell/>`_ (2 books), and `russell <https://geometor.github.io/russell/>`_ (2 books), backed by **8,430+ tracked 300-DPI primary-source folio scans**.

------------------------------------------------------------

* **Expanded Golden Ratio & Polyhedral Catalogs**: Added `polyhedra <https://geometor.github.io/polyhedra/solids/>`_ (*Catalog of Polyhedra & Leonardo's 1509 Plates*), `huntley <https://geometor.github.io/huntley/catalog/>`_ (*The Divine Proportion*), `livio <https://geometor.github.io/livio/catalog/>`_ (*The Golden Ratio & Myth Triage*), and `posamentier <https://geometor.github.io/posamentier/catalog/>`_ (*The Glorious Golden Ratio*), alongside `elements <https://geometor.github.io/elements/elements2/>`_, `etc <https://geometor.github.io/etc/centers/>`_, `wildberger <https://geometor.github.io/wildberger/topics/>`_, `fundamentals <https://geometor.github.io/fundamentals/topics/>`_, `divine <https://geometor.github.io/divine/constructions/>`_, `knott <https://geometor.github.io/knott/catalog/>`_, and `codes <https://geometor.github.io/codes/systems/>`_.

Changelog¶

0.3.0¶

2026-11-12

  • Expanded the GEOMETOR Projects Atlas and Masters & Lineage gallery from 24 to 46 public repositories, distinguishing Scholarly Editions (*Book Codices*) of public-domain treatises from Mathematical Catalogs of modern constructive research (etc, wildberger, divine, knott, codes).

  • Added Wave 2 Book Codices and Catalogs across all pillars: codes, byrne, archimedes, apollonius, heron, menelaus, ptolemy, ceva, feuerbach, staudt, petersen, casey, lemoine, klein, plato, nicomachus, fibonacci, cusa, durer, kepler, poincare, thompson, and knott, plus expanded euclid (Data), pappus (Synagoge), pacioli (Summa 1494), divine (Cut-the-Knot Golden Constructions), and altshiller-court (v0.3.0).

0.2.0¶

2026-11-11

  • Complete public threshold refresh for geometor.com on the foundation Scriptorium theme.

  • Added exact symbolic SymPy interactive hero figure (vesica_to_phi.svg) stepping from two starting points through the Vesica Piscis and Equidistant Poles to George Odom’s Golden Section (\(\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\)).

  • Revived and modernized foundational geometric essays (Field & Points, Lines & Spreads, Circles & Quadrance, The Golden Ratio, Squaring the Circle & the Pyramids) and the full Masters & Lineage roster from Euclid, Aristotle, Proclus, and Pappus through Aquinas, Pacioli, Kepler, Pascal, Hilbert, Bertrand Russell, Hoenen, Altshiller-Court, Seelig, Walter & Lao Russell, Jean, Kimberling, and Wildberger.

  • Added comprehensive Projects Atlas across all five GEOMETOR pillars (24 repositories and live Book Codices) and the @geometor YouTube Broadcast Studio showcase.

2023-11-15

  • Repository initialized.

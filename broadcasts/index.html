The @geometor Visual Proof & Harmonic Broadcast Studio

Every proposition and triangle center in GEOMETOR is more than static ink: it is a temporal unfolding. On @geometor on YouTube, our automated rendering studio translates exact symbolic geometor.model constructions into dual-format QHD (`2560×1440`) widescreen broadcasts and vertical Shorts accompanied by pure-interval acoustic cues.

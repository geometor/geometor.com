About¶

Architecture Is Order

GEOMETOR is created and stewarded by phi ARCHITECT.

Trained in architecture, design systems, and symbolic software, phi ARCHITECT launched GEOMETOR from a conviction shared by builders and natural philosophers across millennia: architecture is order—and geometry is the primary discipline through which order becomes intelligible, measurable, and shareable.

From the earliest interactive browser experiments in GEOMETOR Explorer to the multi-language Book Codices of Euclid, Proclus, Aquinas, Pacioli, Hilbert, Hoenen, Altshiller-Court, Kimberling, and Wildberger—and onward into 3D living phyllotaxis studios, neuro-symbolic program synthesis, and QHD harmonic broadcasts—every piece of the workshop is built openly on Linux using Python, SymPy, SVG, Three.js, and the PHOTON Platform.

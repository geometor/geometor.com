Classical Antiquity: Demonstration, Elements & Projection
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Aristotle · Euclid · Pappus of Alexandria · Proclus Lycius

----

Aristotle (384–322 BC)
----------------------

* **GEOMETOR Edition**: `aristotle — Loci Geometrici et Noetici <https://geometor.github.io/aristotle/>`_ (`Repo <https://github.com/geometor/aristotle>`_)

In *Posterior Analytics*, *Physics*, *De Anima*, and *Metaphysics* IX.9 (1051a21–33), Aristotle formulated the classical theory of **demonstrative science** (*episteme*) and observed how latent geometric relations are brought from potency into luminous actuality **by the act of dividing and constructing the figure**: *"If the lines had already been divided, the theorems would have been obvious as soon as seen."*

----

Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BC)
------------------------------------

* **GEOMETOR Editions**: `euclid <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/>`_ (Heath 13-Book Critical Codex) · `elements <https://geometor.github.io/elements/>`_ (601-Node G-Index) · `YouTube Playlist <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUXlBs_QySgw>`_

Euclid's thirteen books of the *Elements* established the enduring paradigm of deductive architecture: beginning from points, straight lines, and circles (`Book I, Postulates 1–3 <https://geometor.github.io/elements/elements2/G.14/>`_), unfolding congruence, area application, division in **Extreme and Mean Ratio** (*II.11*, *VI.30*), Eudoxus's theory of proportion (*Book V*), commensurability and irrationals (*Books VII–X*), and culminating in the cosmic construction of the **five regular Platonic solids** (*Book XIII*) governed by :math:`\phi`.

----

Pappus of Alexandria (c. 290 – c. 350 AD)
-----------------------------------------

* **GEOMETOR Edition**: `pappus <https://geometor.github.io/pappus/>`_ (`Repo <https://github.com/geometor/pappus>`_)

Last great synthesist of the Alexandrian mathematical school. Book VII of Pappus's *Synagoge* (*Collection*) preserves the ancient treasury of **geometric analysis and synthesis**, invariance of the four-point **cross-ratio** under perspective projection, harmonic division, and **Pappus's Hexagon Theorem** (Prop. 139)—the cornerstone of modern projective geometry, verified across all six :math:`S_3` permutations and Steiner star points in ``geometor/pappus``.

----

Proclus Lycius (412–485 AD)
---------------------------

* **GEOMETOR Edition**: `proclus <https://geometor.github.io/proclus/>`_ (`Repo <https://github.com/geometor/proclus>`_)

Head (*diadochus*) of the Athenian Academy whose *Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements* preserves both Eudemus's lost history of early Greek geometry and a profound philosophy of **mathematical imagination** (*phantasia*). For Proclus, geometric construction is **projection** (*probole*): the discursive intellect projects indivisible ratios onto the extended screen of imagination so that universal form can be contemplated in concrete multiplicity.

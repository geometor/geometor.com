Living Form, Harmonics & Algebraic Geometry Today
=================================================

.. rubric:: Haeckel · Kayser · Walter & Lao Russell · Jean · Odom · Kimberling · Wildberger

----

Ernst Haeckel (1834–1919)
-------------------------

Naturalist, marine biologist, and scientific illustrator whose *Kunstformen der Natur* (*Art Forms in Nature*, 1899–1904) revealed icosahedral, dodecahedral, hexagonal, and logarithmic spiral geometries in radiolarians, diatoms, siphonophores, and calcareous sponges—demonstrating that polyhedral symmetry is an active morphogenetic principle of living organisms.

----

Hans Kayser (1891–1964)
-----------------------

Founder of modern **Pythagorean Harmonics** (*Akróasis: The Theory of World Harmonics*, *Lehrbuch der Harmonik*). Kayser investigated integer and superparticular string ratios, the *lambdoma* matrix, crystallographic proportions, and botanical forms—inspiring GEOMETOR's union of visual construction with pure-interval acoustic cues in `@geometor <https://www.youtube.com/@geometor>`_ studio broadcasts.

----

Walter Russell (1871–1963) & Lao Russell (1904–1988)
----------------------------------------------------

* **GEOMETOR Edition**: `russell — Home Study Course <https://geometor.github.io/russell/>`_ (`Repo <https://github.com/geometor/russell>`_)

Artist, sculptor, and natural philosopher Walter Russell and co-author Lao Russell articulated a geometric wave-field cosmogony built on the **interpenetrating cube and sphere**—modeling compression and expansion as rhythmic optical projections between cubic zero-curvature boundaries and spherical wave-centers across 180 reconstructed vector figures.

----

George Odom (1941–2010)
-----------------------

* **Featured on**: :doc:`../index` (*Interactive Hero Construction*)

Artist and geometer who discovered one of the simplest golden section constructions in history (published as Problem E3007 in the *American Mathematical Monthly*, 1983, proved by H. S. M. Coxeter): joining the midpoints :math:`A` and :math:`B` of two sides of an equilateral triangle and extending :math:`[A B]` to its circumcircle cuts the chord in **Extreme and Mean Ratio** :math:`\phi`—the exact climax of the Vesica-to-:math:`\phi` figure on the threshold of ``geometor.com``.

----

Roger V. Jean
-------------

* **Featured in**: `phyllotaxis <https://geometor.github.io/phyllotaxis/>`_

Mathematician whose treatise *Phyllotaxis: A Systemic Study in Plant Morphogenesis* (Cambridge University Press, 1994) unified continued-fraction divergence theory, visible opposed parastichy pairs, allometric growth matrices, and entropy-minimization models—directly informing the symbolic solvers and 2D/3D WebGL meristem studios in ``geometor/phyllotaxis``.

----

Clark Kimberling & *Forum Geometricorum*
----------------------------------------

* **GEOMETOR Edition**: `etc — Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers <https://geometor.github.io/etc/>`_ (`Repo <https://github.com/geometor/etc>`_ · `YouTube Playlist <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKVZJpKGr5zc>`_)

Through the *Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers* (*ETC*, initiated 1994/1998 and now surpassing **53,000 centers**) and the open-access journal *Forum Geometricorum* (founded by Paul Yiu), Clark Kimberling and collaborators transformed classical triangle geometry into a systematic algebraic discipline—modeled across 4,000+ compiled centers and 16,000+ interactive SVGs in ``geometor/etc``.

----

Norman J. Wildberger
--------------------

* **GEOMETOR Edition**: `wildberger — Divine Proportions <https://geometor.github.io/wildberger/>`_ (`Repo <https://github.com/geometor/wildberger>`_) · ``geometor.model.invariants``

Author of *Divine Proportions: Rational Trigonometry to Universal Geometry* (2005). By replacing distance with **quadrance** :math:`Q` and transcendental angle with **spread** :math:`s`, Wildberger stripped trigonometry of infinite power series and floating-point approximations, allowing Euclidean, relativistic, and projective geometries—as well as ADE graph mutation dynamics—to be computed purely algebraically over arbitrary fields.

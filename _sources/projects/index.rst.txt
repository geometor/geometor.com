Projects
========

.. rubric:: Searchable Medallion Directory of All 64 GEOMETOR Repositories

Explore all **64 public GEOMETOR repositories** across our five architectural pillars—each represented by its own **compass-and-straightedge medallion** (zero Bézier curves). Filter by **pillar** or **language**, search by name or topic, and switch between card, banner, or list views.

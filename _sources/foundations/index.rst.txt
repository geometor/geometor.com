Foundations
===========

.. rubric:: Primitives, Proportions, Rational Invariants & the Golden Ratio

Before constructing 465 propositions of Euclid or 4,000 triangle centers, **GEOMETOR** examines the elementary primitives from which every figure arises: an unmarked **planar field**, **two starting points**, the **line** as linear proportion, the **circle** as perpendicular rotation, and **Extreme and Mean Ratio** (:math:`\phi`) as the self-similar bridge between addition and multiplication.

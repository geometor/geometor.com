Scholarly Editions & Mathematical Catalogs: The 46-Repository Canon
===================================================================

*November 12, 2026* — With release ``v0.3.0``, **GEOMETOR** completes its Wave 2 expansion to **46 interconnected repositories**, establishing a clear architectural complementarity between **Scholarly Editions (*Book Codices*)** for classical treatises and **Mathematical Catalogs** for contemporary mathematical corpora.

1. Twenty-Two New & Expanded Classical Book Codices
---------------------------------------------------

* **Alexandrian & Classical Canon**: `plato <https://geometor.github.io/plato/>`_ (*Meno*, *Republic*, *Timaeus*), `archimedes <https://geometor.github.io/archimedes/>`_ (*Opera Geometrica*), `apollonius <https://geometor.github.io/apollonius/>`_ (*Conics* & *On Tangencies*), `heron <https://geometor.github.io/heron/>`_ (*Metrica* & *Catoptrica*), `menelaus <https://geometor.github.io/menelaus/>`_ (*Sphaerica*), `nicomachus <https://geometor.github.io/nicomachus/>`_ (*Introduction to Arithmetic*), `ptolemy <https://geometor.github.io/ptolemy/>`_ (*Almagest* Book I & *Planisphaerium*), expanded `euclid <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/>`_ (*Data* & *Optics*), and expanded `pappus <https://geometor.github.io/pappus/>`_ (*Synagoge* Books III–VIII).
* **Medieval, Renaissance & Celestial Geometry**: `fibonacci <https://geometor.github.io/fibonacci/>`_ (*Liber Abaci* & *Practica Geometriae*), `cusa <https://geometor.github.io/cusa/>`_ (*De Docta Ignorantia*), expanded `pacioli <https://geometor.github.io/pacioli/summa_1494/>`_ (*Summa* 1494), `durer <https://geometor.github.io/durer/>`_ (*Underweysung der Messung* 1525), `kepler <https://geometor.github.io/kepler/>`_ (*Mysterium*, *Strena*, & *Harmonices Mundi*), and `ceva <https://geometor.github.io/ceva/>`_ (*Statica Constructio* 1678).
* **Modern Triangle, Projective, Visual & Morphological Classics**: `feuerbach <https://geometor.github.io/feuerbach/>`_ (Ceva–Euler–Feuerbach trilogy), `byrne <https://geometor.github.io/byrne/>`_ (1847 polychrome visual pseudocode), `staudt <https://geometor.github.io/staudt/>`_ (*Geometrie der Lage* 1847), `petersen <https://geometor.github.io/petersen/>`_ (*Methods and Theories* 1879), `casey <https://geometor.github.io/casey/>`_ (*Sequel to Euclid* 1888), `lemoine <https://geometor.github.io/lemoine/>`_ (*Géométrographie* 1902), `klein <https://geometor.github.io/klein/>`_ (*Erlangen Program* & *Famous Problems*), `poincare <https://geometor.github.io/poincare/>`_ (*Science and Hypothesis*), `thompson <https://geometor.github.io/thompson/>`_ (*On Growth and Form* 1917), and reparsed one-item-per-page `altshiller-court <https://geometor.github.io/altshiller-court/>`_ (*College Geometry* 1925).

2. Five One-Item-Per-Page Symbolic Mathematical Catalogs
--------------------------------------------------------

For living encyclopedias and copyrighted modern works, GEOMETOR documents the underlying mathematical facts **one item per page** using 100% original prose, ``SymPy`` proofs, and ``geometor.model`` vector diagrams:

* `codes <https://geometor.github.io/codes/>`_ — **Unified Cross-Corpus Notation & Symbolic Code Registry**
* `divine <https://geometor.github.io/divine/constructions/>`_ — **Catalog of Golden Ratio Constructions** (Euclid, Ptolemy, Odom, Hofstetter, Hung, Bataille, *Cut-the-Knot*)
* `knott <https://geometor.github.io/knott/>`_ — **Catalog of Ron Knott's Fibonacci Numbers, the Golden Section & Wythoff Geometry**
* `etc <https://geometor.github.io/etc/>`_ — **Catalog of Clark Kimberling's Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers** (expanded with 9 classical structural theorems & Euler/Nagel/Brocard/Gergonne lines)
* `wildberger <https://geometor.github.io/wildberger/>`_ — **Catalog of Norman J. Wildberger's Rational Trigonometry**

About
=====

.. rubric:: Architecture Is Order

**GEOMETOR** is created and stewarded by `phi ARCHITECT <https://github.com/phiarchitect>`_.

Trained in architecture, design systems, and symbolic software, **phi ARCHITECT** launched GEOMETOR from a conviction shared by builders and natural philosophers across millennia: **architecture is order**—and geometry is the primary discipline through which order becomes intelligible, measurable, and shareable.

From the earliest interactive browser experiments in *GEOMETOR Explorer* to the multi-language **Book Codices** of Euclid, Proclus, Aquinas, Pacioli, Hilbert, Hoenen, Altshiller-Court, Kimberling, and Wildberger—and onward into 3D living phyllotaxis studios, neuro-symbolic program synthesis, and QHD harmonic broadcasts—every piece of the workshop is built openly on Linux using Python, `SymPy <https://www.sympy.org/>`_, SVG, Three.js, and the `PHOTON Platform <https://github.com/photon-platform>`_.

Meno
====

.. image:: /_static/heroes/plato.svg
   :alt: Meno

Socrates demonstrates mathematical recollection (anamnesis) on the slave boy by doubling a square via its diagonal (82b–85b), defines figure as the limit of solid (75d–76a), and introduces the geometer's method of hypothesis (86e–87b).

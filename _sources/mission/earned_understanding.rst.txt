Earned Understanding & Geometric Noetics
========================================

.. rubric:: Projection, Selection, and Intuitive Formal Abstraction

How does the human intellect—or a reasoning machine—arrive at *universal, necessary* geometric truth from a single constructed diagram?

This epistemological question stands at the heart of GEOMETOR's **Historical & Axiomatic Scriptorium**, tracing a continuous 2,300-year conversation from antiquity to twentieth-century foundations:

1. **Aristotle** (`aristotle <https://geometor.github.io/aristotle/>`_): In *Posterior Analytics* and *Metaphysics*, scientific knowledge (*episteme*) requires grasping the *reasoned fact* (*to dioti*)—seeing *why* the angles of a triangle equal two right angles the instant the parallel or exterior construction is drawn (*Metaph.* IX.9, 1051a21–33).
2. **Proclus** (`proclus <https://geometor.github.io/proclus/>`_): In his *Commentary on Book I of Euclid's Elements*, the geometric imagination (*phantasia*) serves as an active screen onto which the understanding (*dianoia*) **projects** (*probole*) pure unextended ratios so that the intellect can inspect their divisions and symmetries.
3. **Thomas Aquinas** (`aquinas <https://geometor.github.io/aquinas/>`_): In *Super Boetium De Trinitate* (qq. 5–6), mathematical science abstracts from sensible matter while retaining **intelligible matter** (*materia intelligibilis*)—the pure continuum of extension in which distinct circles and triangles can be instantiated, compared, and intersected, always terminating (*judicium*) in imaginative construction.
4. **Hilbert & Bertrand Russell** (`hilbert <https://geometor.github.io/hilbert/>`_, `bertrand-russell <https://geometor.github.io/bertrand-russell/>`_): At the turn of the twentieth century, Hilbert's *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (1899) and Russell's *Foundations of Geometry* (1897) stripped away unstated diagrammatic assumptions, isolating incidence, order, congruence, parallel, and continuity axioms and testing their logical independence through explicit algebraic models.
5. **Petrus Hoenen, S.J.** (`hoenen <https://geometor.github.io/hoenen/>`_): In *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954), Hoenen answered pure formalism by recovering **intuitive formal abstraction**: axioms are not arbitrary rules of a symbol game, because the necessity of Euclidean nexus is *first apprehended directly within concrete spatial construction* before being codified into formal postulates.

Projection and Selection in ``geometor.model``
----------------------------------------------

GEOMETOR translates this classical epistemology directly into software architecture:

* **Projection (*Probole*)**: Constructing ``[ A B ]`` or ``( A B )`` projects a continuous geometric law across the entire plane.
* **Selection (*Judicium*)**: Intersecting two projections ``E₁ ∩ E₂`` selects exact algebraic points whose coordinates and **ancestral dependency DAG** record every postulate and prior locus required to bring them into being.
* **Earned Understanding in AI (`seer`)**: Rather than training opaque neural networks to guess pixel grids directly, `geometor/seer <https://geometor.github.io/seer/>`_ pairs hypothesis imagination (*Dreamer*) with verifiable symbolic Python synthesis (*Coder*)—requiring every proposed insight to earn its validity against exact structural constraints.

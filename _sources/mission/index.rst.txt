Mission
=======

.. rubric:: Gather Knowledge · Uncover Patterns · Share Wonder

**GEOMETOR** is dedicated to exploring the ordering principles of form, proportion, and mind—beginning with the simplest compass-and-straightedge relationships on an unmarked plane and following them wherever exact deductive necessity leads.

.. picture:: ../_static/svg/root3-harmonics.svg
   :alt: Root-3 and Vesica Piscis harmonic field geometry

Motivation: Where Arithmetic and Geometry Meet
----------------------------------------------

Since antiquity, geometers, natural philosophers, and architects have marveled at a remarkably simple relationship between two unequal magnitudes :math:`a` and :math:`b`—that *the lesser is to the greater as the greater is to the whole*:

.. math::

   \frac{a}{b} = \frac{b}{a + b}

Out of this single proportion—Euclid's *division in extreme and mean ratio*, Luca Pacioli's *Divina Proportione*, and modern mathematics' Golden Ratio :math:`\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}`—unfold principles of self-similarity, harmonic division, polyhedral symmetry, and living growth that are staggering in their coherence.

The Golden Ratio behaves as an **inflection point between arithmetic and geometry**. It emerges naturally at the intersection of unlike proportions—where equilateral (:math:`\sqrt{3}`) and orthogonal (:math:`\sqrt{2}`) symmetries meet not in discord, but in harmony.

The Threefold Motion
--------------------

Every initiative within GEOMETOR participates in three complementary movements:

1. **Gather Knowledge**
   Reconstruct the foundational canon of geometric, projective, rational, and epistemological thought—Euclid, Aristotle, Pappus, Proclus, Aquinas, Pacioli, Kepler, Hilbert, Bertrand Russell, Altshiller-Court, Hoenen, Seelig, Walter & Lao Russell, Kimberling, and Wildberger—in clean, multi-track **Book Codices** cross-referenced down to the individual proposition and figure.

2. **Uncover Patterns**
   Build an exact **assembly language for constructive geometry** (`geometor.model <https://github.com/geometor/model>`_, `geometor.divine <https://github.com/geometor/divine>`_, `geometor.elements <https://github.com/geometor/elements>`_) capable of constructing thousands of symbolic loci without floating-point drift, tracking full ancestral dependency graphs, and automatically detecting emergent golden sections, harmonic ranges, and projective collineations.

3. **Share Wonder**
   Give pure logic a visible and audible form. Every diagram, interactive WebGL meristem, print folio, and dual-format broadcast on `@geometor <https://www.youtube.com/@geometor>`_ treats rigorous proof as an object of contemplation, clarity, and beauty.

Ethos
-----

The pursuit of unequivocal mathematical truth is a discipline of patience, precision, and humility. GEOMETOR seeks to:

- **Share knowledge openly** in durable, inspectable formats;
- **Require exactness**, never mistaking a decimal approximation for an algebraic proof;
- **Bridge epochs and disciplines**, uniting classical synthetic prose, rational algebra, botanical morphogenesis, and machine program synthesis;
- **Remain humble** before the depth of order uncovered in even the simplest geometric figure.

Books & Catalogs¶

Complete Index of GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium Editions & Mathematical Catalogs

Across 39 author scriptoria and 11 constructive catalog archives, GEOMETOR publishes 74 multi-track historical Book Codices and 11 symbolic Mathematical Catalogs—each grounded in exact SymPy algebra, archival folio provenance, and calibrated theme-adaptive .euclid-graphic vector diagrams.

  • Mathematical Catalogs (11): Structured one-item-per-page symbolic compendia documenting classical dependency graphs, triangle centers, rational trigonometry laws, polyhedra, golden ratio constructions, Fibonacci identities, and geometric theorem-proving engines.

  • Historical & Philosophical Book Codices (74 Books · 39 Authors): Facing-language critical editions (Greek, Latin, Italian, Early New High German, German, French, Danish/English) preserving verbatim historical primary texts alongside scholarly summaries, notes, concordances, and over 8,400 tracked 300-DPI primary-source folio scans.
